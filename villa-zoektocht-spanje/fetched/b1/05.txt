Jávea is a coastal town bathed by the Mediterranean Sea, located in the north of the province of Alicante. We have good communications and all kinds of services such as supermarkets, leisure premises, restaurants, tennis club, golf course and educational centres. 
It is one of the towns where you can enjoy more than 300 days a year of sun, an ideal climate to enjoy its paradisiacal beaches and coves, as well as its historical centre. 
Villas for sale in Jávea 
If you are looking for relaxation, good weather and an area with all the features you might need, Javea is one of the best areas if you are looking to sell villas. Whether you are a mountain lover or more into the blue of the sea, you will discover a wide variety of villas for sale in Jávea. 
We have villas for sale in different areas of Jávea, both at the foot of the Montgó, a natural park that guards the town, and villas in its 20 kilometres of sea that bathes the coast of Jávea, from the Cova Tallada to the Cala Granadella. 
Villas for Sale in Jávea with sea views 
At MoraguesPons we have a wide variety of villas for sale in Jávea with sea views. As we commented in previous lines, in Jávea we have 20 kilometres of coastline bathed by the blue Mediterranean Sea, which is why we have villas for sale in the famous coves of Granadella, Cala Portitchol, Cala Blanca or in areas such as the port or Cape San Antonio. 
Urbanisations of Javea with Villas for sale 
The Mediterranean Sea is responsible for the incredible sunrises you can enjoy from any villa for sale in Jávea, as it enters any of the windows of these villas to give you one of the most spectacular sunrises in Spain every morning 
Villas for sale in Javea Playa, Montañar-Playa del Arenal 
The Arenal Beach and Montañar area is one of Javea's best known areas for its wide variety of shops, services and restaurants and leisure facilities. The David Ferrer promenade and the Montañar area will offer you one of the best social lives you can imagine. A multitude of supermarkets, clothes shops and a great variety of gastronomic offers make this area one of the best locations to buy a villa for sale. 
Villas for sale in Montgó, Javea

Villas for sale in Portichol, Jávea 
The Portichol is one of the most fashionable areas of Javea, because of its Cala Portitchol or Barraca and its incredible views of the Portichol Island, make this area one of the most spectacular locations to choose from for one of the villas for sale in Javea on the seafront. 
Villas for sale in La Granadella, Javea 
Cala Granadella gives its name to this coastal area. An area with a rocky coastline where you can find various types of properties, especially luxury villas for sale in Jávea , as its majestic cliff areas and incredible views make this area an ideal location for design and luxury villas. 
Villas for sale in Cap Martí, Jávea 
Cap Martí is one of the quietest areas of Jávea, an area where finding villas for sale in Jávea will be a very easy task, as we have villas with all kinds of features. 
Villas for sale in Partida Comunes - Adsubia, Javea 
Partida Comunes- Adsubia is one of the highest areas of Jávea, which is why from its villas for sale you will discover incredible views. Large plots, villas with all kinds of amenities and needs.
