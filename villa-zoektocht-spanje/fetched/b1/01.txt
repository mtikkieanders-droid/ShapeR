Description
Very spacious villa with southwest orientation giving it a panoramic sea view, on clear days even Ibiza and Formentera are clearly visible. The house is located near the coast and the beach of Cala Ambolo.
The living area is spread over 2 levels, the ground floor offers a beautiful entrance, a spacious living and dining room, 3 bedrooms, a renovated kitchen, 2 bathrooms and an open space, which is currently used as an office. On the top floor we find a fabulous master bedroom, very spacious with its own living room with fireplace, ensuite bathroom, a private balcony with again beautiful sea views and a private staircase to the pool.
Outside we find the naya, a large covered terrace with an outdoor kitchen and connected by a few steps to the pool terrace. Around the house a mature garden with even more seating options and besides the garage 2 more parking spaces on site, one of which is covered by a carport. Furthermore a large room that could possibly be used as a separate guest house but now serves as a gym and storage space.
The entire house has aluminium double glazing, air conditioning (hot & cold) and central heating. Also equipped with solar panels with batteries to store electricity and a heat pump for heating the pool.
